CashTrack: Personal Finance

100% On-Device Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026 | Last Updated: November 11, 2026

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

CashTrack is designed from the ground up to be a privacy-first, local-only personal finance tracker. Unlike traditional finance apps that transmit your banking history to remote cloud servers, data brokers, or advertising networks, CashTrack performs 100% of transaction parsing, categorization, and ledger storage directly on your physical device.

2. Information Collection & Usage

  • Financial Transaction SMS (With Prominent Disclosure & Consent): Only after you review our in-app Prominent Disclosure and grant explicit runtime consent, CashTrack reads device SMS alerts strictly to detect bank debits, credit card spends, UPI confirmations, and EMI payments. Non-financial messages (personal chats, marketing spam, standalone OTPs) are completely ignored and discarded in memory. All parsing runs locally on-device; raw SMS messages never leave your phone. Users may decline SMS permission and track expenses manually or via Gmail sync.
  • Optional Gmail Integration & Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance: If you connect a Google account to import bank email alerts, authentication occurs directly through official Google OAuth2. Token exchanges and email body fetching communicate strictly between your device and Google's official Gmail APIs. No third-party intermediate server ever has access to your credentials or emails. CashTrack's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Email data is processed strictly on-device to extract financial transaction amounts and merchant names and is never shared, transferred, or sold.
  • Optional Community Merchant Category Directory: Users may optionally share anonymized public brand-to-category mappings (for example, "Swiggy → Food & Dining") with an automatic 24-hour rate guard and personal-number PII scrubber. No amounts, account numbers, timestamps, SMS bodies, or personal identifiers are ever transmitted.
  • Hardware-Backed SQLCipher Local Database: All accounts, categories, budgets, and transactions are stored locally in an AES-256 encrypted SQLCipher database wrapped by Android KeyStore.

3. Zero Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your financial records to any third parties, advertisers, lenders, or data analytics companies. There are no advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, or behavioral profiling services embedded in the application.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion

You maintain complete ownership of your data at all times:

  • Export: You can export your full transaction ledger as encrypted backups, PDF statements, or CSV spreadsheets at any time.
  • Delete: You can wipe individual transactions or clear the entire database with a single tap in the app's settings.
  • Revocation: You can revoke SMS, notification, or Google account permissions at any time via Android system settings.

5. Security Safeguards

CashTrack implements military-grade security mechanisms on your phone:

  • Biometric App Lock: Protects app launch with fingerprint, face unlock, or device PIN.
  • FLAG_SECURE: Prevents accidental screen captures and hides app preview in Android's recent apps switcher.
  • AES-256-GCM: Encrypts backup files with PBKDF2 key derivation.
